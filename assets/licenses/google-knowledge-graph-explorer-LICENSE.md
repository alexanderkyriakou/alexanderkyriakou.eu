### Google Enterprise Knowledge Graph Basic

The explorer retrieves entity records from Google Enterprise Knowledge Graph Basic. Returned names, types, identifiers, descriptions, websites, source references and images are source material rather than original software or prose authored by Alexander Kyriakou. Their display and reuse remain subject to applicable Google service terms and the rights and terms of underlying sources. This includes source material included in CSV exports.

- Service documentation: https://docs.cloud.google.com/enterprise-knowledge-graph/docs/overview
- Search API documentation: https://docs.cloud.google.com/enterprise-knowledge-graph/docs/search-api
- Identifier documentation: https://docs.cloud.google.com/enterprise-knowledge-graph/docs/mid

The explorer is an independent project. Google is not identified as its developer, publisher or sponsor. Google names and marks remain with their respective rights holders.
